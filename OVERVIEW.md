Windows 2000 adds a light theme built from the exact colors of the Windows 2000 "Windows Standard" scheme: the #D4D0C8 button face as the app background, white window wells for the sidebar, cards and inputs, #808080 button shadow for borders, and #0A246A highlight navy as the accent. Select it under Settings, Appearance, Theme. Paseo themes set colors only, so fonts, rounded corners and spacing stay Paseo's, and the 3D bevels and title bar gradients of Windows 2000 are not drawn. Paseo derives the remaining shades from these colors, so text on the accent renders in #D4D0C8 rather than white, and hovered and selected sidebar rows share the same gray. Requires Paseo 0.15.3 or later.
