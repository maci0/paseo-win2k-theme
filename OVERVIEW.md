Windows 2000 turns Paseo into a Windows 2000 desktop. On desktop and web, the sidebar and workspace become raised windows on the #3A6EA5 desktop blue, with navy-to-light-blue title bars that go grey when the other window has focus. Buttons, tabs, menus, dialogs and scrollbars get the 3D bevels, text fields and your prompts sit in sunken white wells, selections turn highlight navy with white text, and the pointer uses the Windows 2000 arrow, hand and I-beam. Colors are the exact "Windows Standard" registry defaults: #D4D0C8 button face, white windows, #808080 shadows and #0A246A highlight.

Select it under Settings, Appearance, Theme. Interface text uses Tahoma where it is installed, which overrides the interface font setting while this theme is selected; otherwise your platform font stays. The iOS and Android apps get the colors only. The window chrome hooks into Paseo's own element names, was built against Paseo 0.15.3, and parts of it may stop applying after a Paseo update changes those names. Requires Paseo 0.15.3 or later.
